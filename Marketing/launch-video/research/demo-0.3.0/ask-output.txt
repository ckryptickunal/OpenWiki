When starting out, you should identify who to email by following these steps:

*   **Analyze existing data:** Look at your current customers, specifically those who signed the contract, paid, or pushed the deal to close, and target people with similar job profiles [1].
*   **Identify the pain point:** If you do not have customers yet, ask yourself who in an organization has the most to gain from buying your software [1].
*   **Leverage your network:** Reach out to people you have previously worked or interned with, or look for individuals online whose work you respect and who could provide helpful feedback [6].

The provided passages do not cover who to email if you have no existing network, customers, or clear organizational beneficiaries for your product.
