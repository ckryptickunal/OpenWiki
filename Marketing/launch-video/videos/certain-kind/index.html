there’s a certain kind of person
who watches the whole talk
who reads the essay twice
who writes it all down at 2 a.m.
and can’t find it by morning
who remembers the idea
but not where it was
who builds with an AI beside them
and wants it to know what they know
every talk.
every essay.
every note.
becomes a page. linked. searchable. yours.
for the ones who keep learning
and never want to lose a thing
>OW
