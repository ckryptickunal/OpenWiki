# Brag Plan: OpenWiki

## What is this app?
OpenWiki is an open-source Python CLI that turns YouTube videos, essays, and personal notes into a linked, Obsidian-ready Markdown knowledge base the user owns.

## The angle
Saved content is not knowledge until it connects. The film begins with a quiet, pointed truth—“Your bookmarks are not a knowledge base.”—then shows OpenWiki doing the useful work: bringing sources in, running one command, and producing a navigable web of plain Markdown pages. The tone borrows the project's warm desk, paper, handwritten wordmark, and archival photography instead of generic SaaS chrome.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On warm paper, a small stack of disconnected source cards lands one by one. Large dark-brown type resolves to: “Your bookmarks are not a knowledge base.” The line holds long enough to land.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Real source types—YouTube, blogs, and notes—fan into a focused terminal window.
- The real `openwiki ingest --all` command types in and resolves into source, person, company, and topic Markdown pages connected by `[[wikilinks]]`.
- The finished knowledge graph opens as an Obsidian-ready vault, while “plain Markdown · local or cloud LLM · yours” confirms the ownership story.

## Outro / punchline
The handwritten OpenWiki name writes itself beside the real app icon. Final line: “Turn what you save into what you know.” A small “Free · open source · MIT” pill lands below.

## User flow worth showing
Bring in a YouTube video, essay, or note → run `openwiki ingest --all` → open a linked Markdown wiki / Obsidian vault with source, entity, and topic pages.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet editorial product film on a sunlit research desk
- Interpretation: Confident, spacious pacing; tactile paper-and-card motion; no hype language; animation feels precise and gently physical.

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`; steady, clean, and understated for a polished launch film
- Music treatment: begin near the start at a low supportive level, let the first confident beat introduce the hook, and fade cleanly through the last 0.8s
- Music cue guidance: preset available at 109.96 BPM; target 8.74s for the linked-wiki payoff, 13.11s for the ownership scene, and 17.47s for the final brand lockup; use the 3.82–6.56s beat window for terminal/source accents
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; allow the paper glow and graph links to breathe a few percent with musical energy, never display a waveform
- SFX posture: sparse; soft card placements, restrained key ticks, one quiet confirmation tone
- Audio-coupled moments: source cards land one by one; command types; linked pages connect sequentially; final logo pill lands
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, booms, trailer hits, or effects that overpower the editorial calm

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Not a knowledge base — 3.2s
Three disconnected source cards—YouTube, an essay, and a handwritten note—settle onto the warm surface. The headline resolves: “Your bookmarks are not a knowledge base.” Use actual OpenWiki imagery and visual language.
Sequential/interaction: yes — source cards arrive one by one quickly, then the complete hook holds for at least 1.5s
Audio intent: a quiet opening with tactile card placements and a confident musical lock-in
Audio-coupled idea: each card placement gets a soft paper/card sound; the headline settles on the 1.60s strong cue
Music: warm, lightly rhythmic bed
Transition mood: soft, clean slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Bring anything in — 4.0s
A refined terminal card appears between the sources. Real commands type in: `openwiki youtube <url>` followed by `openwiki ingest --all`. Small labels confirm “YouTube”, “blogs”, and “your notes”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the cursor types the two commands; labels settle and remain readable
Audio intent: purposeful forward motion with very restrained key ticks
Audio-coupled idea: typing and cursor confirmation; terminal reveal near the 5.80s cue
Music: warm, lightly rhythmic bed
Transition mood: precise paper wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Pages become a wiki — 5.0s
The terminal compresses into a source page. Entity and topic pages unfold around it like index cards, then `[[wikilinks]]` draw between them. Use realistic titles from the included demo content but no personal/private data. A small label reads: “sources · people · companies · topics”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — source page first, then entity/topic cards, then links; the completed graph holds for at least 1.5s
Audio intent: the satisfying payoff—the system becomes legible
Audio-coupled idea: soft card placements for pages and a restrained confirmation tone when the links complete
Music: warm, optimistic bed
Transition mood: slow depth move → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Plain files. Real ownership. — 4.3s
The linked cards glide into an Obsidian-style vault window over the real warm desk image. Three concise truths appear with generous holds: “Plain Markdown.” “Works with Gemini, OpenAI, or Ollama.” “Your files stay yours.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three truths appear every other beat and remain together on screen
Audio intent: confident and calm, with one soft accent on the final ownership line
Audio-coupled idea: subtle line accents; no rapid text percussion
Music: warm bed opening slightly toward the outro
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — OpenWiki — 3.5s
The actual app icon settles beside the handwritten “openwiki” wordmark as it writes on. Final line: “Turn what you save into what you know.” A small pill reads “Free · open source · MIT”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — icon, wordmark, then tagline and pill; all hold cleanly for the final second
Audio intent: gentle resolution with a small logo confirmation and musical fade
Audio-coupled idea: wordmark write-on follows the music phrase; final pill lands softly
Music: warm bed fading out over the final 0.8s
Transition mood: final hold
